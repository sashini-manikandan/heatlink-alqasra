How HEATLINK works

One front door. Coordinated protection.

01 / CONNECT
A person asks for help

Residents and workers use a simple mobile-friendly service, with multilingual access and low-data design.

02 / ROUTE
The request reaches the right team

In a live system, verified requests are routed to health, municipal, utility or labour-protection partners.

03 / CLOSE THE LOOP
Support is confirmed

Authorised responders update the case, record the outcome and escalate unresolved high-risk requests.
